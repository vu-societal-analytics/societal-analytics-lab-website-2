Mahdi Rahimi – Lab Research Engineer at the Societal Analytics Lab.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Engineer at the Societal Analytics Lab. One of my main tasks is working on OPTIMA, the Observatory for Political Texts, Images, and Multimedia, an open science infrastructure where researchers can securely store, analyze, annotate, and share political communication data. To do this, we are extending and integrating two of the lab’s open-source platforms, AmCAT and AnnoTinder, and adding FAIR data sharing and non-consumptive research for copyrighted or sensitive material. 

I hold a PhD in GIS from K.N. Toosi University of Technology. Before joining VU, I led quantitative analysis and data pipeline development for the Horizon Europe project AccessCity4All at the University of Groningen. My expertise includes research software engineering, data modeling, knowledge graphs, network science, location-based social networks, spatial analysis, and large-scale data pipelines in R, Python, and SQL.
