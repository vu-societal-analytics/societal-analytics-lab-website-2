We promote computational knowledge exchange and foster global connections.

The lab connects people with different skills in computational analysis. Our goal is to help social scientists acquire the computing skills they need, without needing a lot of spare time to do it.

![Illustration of a computational methods pipeline](https://societal-analytics.nl/images/illustrations/CompMethPipeline.png)

## Three ways to learn

Researchers have little time to learn something new, so we offer three options. Pick the one that fits your schedule.

- [Collaboration](#collaboration): ask for expert support or share your own skills.
- [R material](#r-material): a course repository and a book, free to use.
- [R workshops](#r-workshops): courses designed for social scientists.

## Collaboration

Speed up science by exchanging ideas, knowledge and skills.

**Need an expert opinion?** If you need advice or support on a particular topic, let us know and we will help you find the right person.

**Have a skill to share?** If you are a lab member with skills that could benefit others, tell us. We want to build a collaboration network among lab members.

[Get in touch](https://societal-analytics.nl/contact/)

## R material

We maintain a repository and a book with R course material. Topics range from data wrangling to text analysis and advanced statistics. Use them as you please, and contact us with questions or requests.

- [R course material](https://github.com/ccs-amsterdam/r-course-material/tree/master): the course repository on GitHub.
- [Communication Science R Book](https://vu-communication-science.github.io/R-canon/): the same material as an online book.

The material was initially developed by [Wouter van Atteveldt](https://societal-analytics.nl/team/wouter-van-atteveldt/) and [Kasper Welbers](https://societal-analytics.nl/team/kasper-welbers/), and is now also maintained by other lab members.

## R workshops

We offer R workshops designed for social scientists. They were developed by [Sofia Gil-Clavel](https://societal-analytics.nl/team/sofia-gil-clavel/), who has many years of experience teaching R to demographers and social scientists from qualitative and quantitative backgrounds.

[See the course information](https://github.com/SofiaG1l/R_Course/)
