Research software, built in the open, for computational social science

The Societal Analytics Lab is the first point of contact for anyone applying computational methods in social science research. We are research engineers who build and maintain the tools those methods depend on.

The engineering problems we work on

Social science research produces hard software problems. These are the ones we spend our time on.

Search and storage at scale

Indexing and querying large document collections so researchers can validate and explore data quickly.

Annotation that is fast and reliable

Interfaces for coding text and images that reduce effort and error, and produce data you can trust.

Compute for text and image processing

GPU-driven pipelines for automatic analysis, made usable for people who are not infrastructure experts.

Reproducibility by design

Tools and infrastructure that make research transparent, repeatable and shareable.

Who we build for

We fit research to the digital society we live in, and we target two groups of scholars.

New to computational methods

You are curious how these methods could benefit your research. We offer guidance, training and essential tools. Adding computational elements to a grant often makes it more competitive, and we help you design, write and carry out the proposal.

Experienced, but short on resources

You already know the methods and lack the tools or infrastructure. We provide them, and build what is missing.
